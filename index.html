Executive profile

I am a senior public health, nutrition and programme leader. I design, lead and strengthen integrated programmes across Kenya’s health, nutrition, resilience and community systems.

I work with national and county governments, donors, international NGOs, local organizations, health facilities and communities, and I turn technical evidence and policy priorities into practical systems, services and interventions.

My strongest contribution is connecting household realities, community systems, government structures, evidence and policy. I showed this most clearly leading Case Management for Nutrition Resilience (CMNR), where nutrition, health, livelihoods, markets, WASH, climate resilience and protection were built around vulnerable households instead of delivered as separate projects.

How I lead

I design systems that turn strategy into results. Each programme I lead follows the same cycle.
